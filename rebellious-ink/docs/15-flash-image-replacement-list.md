## Image spec (same for every file)

- JPEG, PNG or WebP, under 15 MB. Square works best: the catalogue shows square tiles, and the game result cards crop to a square.
- At least 1200 x 1200 pixels (1600 x 1600 preferred) so the A4 flash sheet PDF and the share images stay sharp.
- Clean white or plain background, design centred with a little space around it, no watermark, no text baked into the picture (the site adds the title and price).
- One design per file. Only the two designs on "Archive sheet C" share a sheet image and are cropped by a hotspot; replace those with single-design files too and the sheet can be retired.

## How to upload

Sign in, go to Owner, then Flash, and use "Add a design" (or an artist can upload under their own Flash page). Pick the collection: Catalogue for the fixed-price wall, Gumball for the six capsule designs. Set the theme, ink and the small and palm prices. Once a new design is live, retire the archive one from the same page so clients only see your work. Ask the desk (me) if you would rather hand over a folder of files and have them loaded in one go.
