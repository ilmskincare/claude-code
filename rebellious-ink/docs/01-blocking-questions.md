# Blocking questions for the owner

Only questions that genuinely change what gets built or whether it can go live. Everything else has a sensible default that is editable in `/owner/settings` or in code configuration.

| # | Question | Why it blocks | Default assumed meanwhile | What it unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is the legal entity behind Rebellious Ink (sole trader, Ltd, partnership), its registered address, and which bank account receives client deposits? | Stripe account ownership, invoice issuer, privacy notice controller, VAT treatment all depend on it. | A placeholder `LegalEntity` named "Rebellious Ink Ltd (TEST — legal entity unconfirmed)". | Stripe live account, correct invoices, final privacy notice. |
| 2 | Does the studio hold the rights to the supplied logo artwork (who designed it, any licence terms)? | The logo is embedded in the site, PDFs and manifest. | Logo used as supplied by the owner in this brief; rights assumed held but unverified. | Public launch, merchandise and marketing exports. |
| 3 | Where are the premises (local authority) and in which UK nation? | Piercing age rules, registration and licensing, local hygiene requirements differ (England/Wales/Scotland/NI, and council by-laws). | England assumed; piercing set to 18+ publicly; no under-18 pathway. | Correct age pathways, licence records, the Phase 2 consent wording. |
| 4 | Permission to create or connect provider accounts: Stripe (test then live), Resend (sending domain), a managed Postgres (e.g. Supabase), Vercel (hosting), object storage bucket. | Nothing can be deployed or send real email or take real payments without them. | None connected. Email goes to a development outbox; card payment is "not connected"; local disk storage. | Staging deployment and end-to-end live test with test keys. |
| 5 | Published deposit, cancellation, reschedule and no-show policy. | The quote terms text and the cancel/deposit-outcome options must match what the studio actually enforces. | Terms v1: deposit deducted from final price, non-refundable under 48 hours or no-show, one free reschedule with 48 hours' notice. | Final `DEFAULT_TERMS` and settings. |
| 6 | Will the studio offer any piercing to under-18s, and under what conditions? | Legally reviewed policy required before any parent/guardian flow exists. | 18+ only for all piercings. | Phase 2 guardian consent pathway (if ever). |
| 7 | Who are the qualified practitioners who will approve the aftercare and preparation guides, and who provides legal review of the consent forms? | Guides are seeded as drafts and render with a "not approved" banner; consent forms will not be built live without sign-off. | Nothing approved. | Removing the draft banner; issuing documents to real clients. |
| 8 | Accountant confirmation of: VAT registration status, VAT on chair rent, treatment of tips and card fees in the eligible base, and HMRC employment-status position for each practitioner. | The calculator and future commission/rent entries must use agreed rules. | Not VAT registered; tips excluded from eligible base; card fees included; everything labelled "to be confirmed". | Phase 3 billing and statements. |
| 9 | Merchant of record for chair-rent artists: do they take their own card payments, or does the studio collect everything and settle? | Decides whether Stripe Connect is needed, who owns the client payment relationship, and who refunds. | Studio collects deposits through its own Stripe account; artists' own sales arrangements recorded only as notes. | Stripe Connect design or a clear "studio collects all" model. |
| 10 | Should this application move to its own repository? It currently lives in `rebellious-ink/` inside the `ilmskincare/claude-code` fork, which is the only repository this session could reach. | CI, deployment hooks and access control are per repository. | Built as a subfolder on branch `feat/rebellious-ink-studio-os`. | A clean repository with Vercel Git integration. |
