### RIDDOR

RIDDOR is the Reporting of Injuries, Diseases and Dangerous Occurrences Regulations 2013. The responsible person (the employer, or the person in control of the premises) must report certain events to the HSE. For the studio, the main ones are:

- **Over-seven-day injury.** A worker is away from work or unable to do their normal work for more than seven consecutive days (not counting the day of the accident) because of an accident at work. Report within 15 days. Absences of more than three days must be recorded, though not reported.
- **Specified injuries and deaths** of workers or serious injuries to members of the public taken from the premises to hospital for treatment.
- **Blood-borne virus exposure.** A sharps injury involving known infected blood or body fluid is a reportable dangerous occurrence under regulation 7. An absence of more than seven days caused by an exposure, and an occupational infection, are also reportable.
- **Diseases** caused by work and certain occupational infections.

Elle Evaa decides whether an event is reportable, using the HSE website at https://www.hse.gov.uk/riddor/, and reports online. Reports are logged in document 10. When in doubt, ask the HSE or the council.
