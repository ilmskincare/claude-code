## Points to know before submitting

- The England position applies throughout. Welsh intimate piercing law (Public Health (Wales) Act 2017, sections 95 and 96) does not apply in Birmingham. The studio rule of 18 and over for intimate piercing, and for all piercing other than ear and nose, is studio policy, not an English legal requirement.
- The CIEH toolkit is from July 2013. Check for a newer UKHSA or CIEH edition before submitting.
- See section 10 of `00-research-notes.md` for the full list of items that could not be confirmed.
