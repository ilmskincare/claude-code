## 1. Purpose

This schedule lists every person who must be registered with Birmingham City Council before they carry out a treatment at Rebellious Ink, and records the premises registration. Birmingham requires registration of both the person and the premises under Part VIII of the Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1982. The council's form takes one practitioner and one treatment type at a time, with a separate application and fee for each, so a person who performs two kinds of treatment has two lines. Nobody treats a client until their registration certificate is in hand and recorded here.

Update this schedule when someone joins, leaves, changes treatment type or moves premises. A practitioner who moves to different premises must submit a new registration.

## 4. Notes

- Fee: the council fee schedule checked on 4 November 2026 lists skin piercing treatment at £80 per application, and a copy licence at £30. Confirm with the council before payment whether a separate premises fee applies.
- Processing: the council aims to process applications within 20 working days, and documents can take up to 10 further working days.
- Duration: registration stays in force until the business ceases or the registration is cancelled (council page, checked 4 November 2026). Record any cancellation or suspension below.
- Display: the certificates and a copy of the byelaws must be displayed prominently at the premises.
- Leavers: when someone leaves, record the date and tell the council licensing team at licensing@birmingham.gov.uk, and keep the certificate details on file.
