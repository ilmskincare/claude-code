## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how the studio makes sure everyone who tattoos or pierces is trained, competent and, where needed, supervised. The CIEH toolkit notes that there is no nationally recognised, accredited course or agreed set of competencies for tattooists and body piercers. The studio therefore sets and checks its own standard, and keeps records so an officer can see them.

## 4. Hepatitis B vaccination and health

Every operator and apprentice is offered hepatitis B vaccination at the studio's cost before they start with clients, as set out in document 02, section 10. The studio keeps a copy of each person's vaccination record or antibody result, with their agreement, and a signed declination form if someone chooses not to be vaccinated. Operators are encouraged to keep tetanus vaccination up to date. These records are health information, are kept confidentially in the locked personnel file, and are seen only by Charlie and Elle Evaa.

## 5. Competence assessment

New operators and apprentices are assessed against a checklist (section 8) by Elle Evaa or another senior artist, who observes them setting up, treating a client, cleaning down and disposing of waste. They are assessed again each year. Concerns are written up and followed with extra training. An operator who cannot show safe practice does not treat clients unsupervised.

Experienced artists joining the studio are asked to give a portfolio, references, and evidence of training. They have an induction in this studio's procedures, and Elle Evaa checks their practice in their first month.

## 6. Apprentices and supervision

Sophie and Honey are apprentices supervised by Elle Evaa. The rules are:

- An apprentice is registered with the council before performing any part of a tattoo on a client [confirm requirement with the council].
- Until an apprentice has been signed off in writing, Elle Evaa is on the premises, in the room or within sight and immediate call, whenever the apprentice works on a client. She checks the design, set-up, skin preparation and technique, and can take over at any point.
- Apprentices progress in stages: observing; set-up, clean-down, stencils and waste handling; practice on practice skin or synthetic materials; supervised work on clients for simple, low-risk work; supervised work on larger or more complex work; and independent work only when Elle Evaa has signed off the stage and Charlie has agreed. Each stage is dated and signed on the apprentice record.
- Clients are told at the start that an apprentice will be involved and who is supervising, and they may decline. The client's consent is recorded.
- Apprentices do not work on under-18 piercings, intimate piercings, or any procedure Elle Evaa has not signed off.
- Apprentices follow all studio policies and are named on the risk assessment.

If Elle Evaa is absent, apprentices do not work on clients unless another registered artist has agreed in writing to supervise them. If there is none, apprentices do cleaning, set-up, and training tasks only.

## 8. Competence checklist (summary)

Observed and signed off by Elle Evaa or another senior artist, with date:

1. Hand washing and gloving correctly.
2. Setting up a sterile work area, opening sterile items without contamination.
3. Skin checks, preparation and stencil or marking.
4. Machine or needle technique, in line with the artist's level.
5. Use of ink caps, batch recording and ink handling.
6. Safe sharps disposal and no recapping.
7. Cleaning down between clients and at the end of the day.
8. Spillage response and sharps injury first aid.
9. Consent, ID check and medical screening.
10. Aftercare explanation and record.
11. Clinical waste handling.
