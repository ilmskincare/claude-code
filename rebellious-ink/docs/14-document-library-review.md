# Document library review sheet (version 1 drafts)

This sheet is for the studio owner and the qualified practitioners who will sign off the client document library. It lists every document in `src/content/documents.ts` (and in `prisma/sql/document-library-v1.sql`), the standard it follows, and what must be checked before approval. There are 23 documents: 3 preparation guides, 6 aftercare guides, 11 information guides and 3 paper-style consent forms.

**Nothing is approved.** Every document is loaded with `approvedAt` empty, so the app shows a Draft banner and says "Do not issue to clients". A document only becomes approved when the studio sets `approvedAt` (and the approver's name) through the app. Until then, none of this wording should be given to a client.

How to use it: for each row, read the document, resolve every "Practitioner to confirm" item, edit the wording to match what the studio actually does, then delete the callout from the body before approving. Each callout appears in the document as a highlighted box so it is easy to find. All documents end with the studio email address, NHS 111 / GP and 999 line.

Standards referred to: the CIEH / Tattoo and Piercing Industry Union "Tattooing and Body Piercing Guidance Toolkit" (2013); the Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1982 Part VIII and local byelaws; the Tattooing of Minors Act 1969; NHS and UKHSA advice; the Association of Professional Piercers aftercare principles; UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018; the Consumer Rights Act 2015. The drafter had no access to the studio's own council byelaws, insurance policy or procedures, so those are always flagged rather than assumed.

## Points that need a decision outside the documents

- **Under-18 piercing.** The Age and ID Policy and the Parental Consent form describe a studio policy (16 and 17 with a parent's signed consent where the council allows, under 16 only with a parent present, no intimate piercing under 18). The website enquiry form and the `piercing.minimumAge` setting currently accept adults only. The policy cannot take effect until the studio approves it and changes both. Until then, "Before Your Piercing" tells under-18s to email first.
- **Tattoos under 18.** The law allows no exceptions and there is no setting for it. The documents say so plainly.
- **Genital piercing.** Check the studio's insurer and local council conditions before offering it, and before approving the genital aftercare guide and the consent form wording for it. The drafter did not research any specific legislation beyond the council byelaws and did not assume any.
- **Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.** The drafting assumes council registration in England under the 1982 Act. Other nations have different licensing and age rules (for example the Public Health (Wales) Act 2017). Confirm which regime applies.
- **Health answers on paper.** A paper form with health answers is special-category personal data. Decide where it is kept, for how long and who may see it. The digital consent in the app stores health answers separately.
- **Cancellation right for online bookings.** Take advice on whether the 14-day cancellation right in the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 applies, and keep the quote terms, the Deposits guide and the website consistent.
- **Where documents appear in the app.** The public library page lists only preparation, aftercare and information guides, so the three consent forms are not listed there (they can still be opened by their key). A booking page only suggests documents whose `procedureType` is `tattoo`, `piercing`, `piercing_ear_face` or `all`. The new `general`, `piercing_oral`, `piercing_body` and `piercing_genital` documents therefore do not appear on a booking page until that filter is extended.
