# Image credits

## Flash catalogue placeholders (staging)

The flash catalogue on staging is filled with crops from six early twentieth-century tattoo flash sheets in the collection of the Smithsonian American Art Museum, released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0, public domain dedication) through the museum's open access programme. They are placeholders until the studio's artists supply their own original designs, and each is titled "(archive)" so nobody mistakes them for current work.

| Smithsonian record | Used for |
|---|---|
| saam_1998.84.50A | Dagger and crescent; Clipper ship |
| saam_1998.84.50B | Lucky cards; Skull and cap |
| saam_1998.84.50C | Moth; Bluebird; Rose; the whole sheet shown in the Sheets view with Butterfly and Swallow and banner as hotspots |
| saam_1998.84.50E | Sailor's grave; Heart and shield |
| saam_1998.84.50F | Winged propeller |
| saam_1998.84.50D | not used (contains a red cross emblem, which is protected in the UK under the Geneva Conventions Act 1957) |

Records: https://collections.si.edu/search/detail/edanmdm:saam_1998.84.50A (and B to F). Licence: CC0. Drawings with national flags, service initials, religious symbols or nudity were deliberately not used.

The crops live in `prisma/seed-assets/flash` so a fresh seed uses them, and `scripts/import-archive-flash.ts` records exactly how they were made.

## Everything else

Logo and brand assets: supplied by the owner. Product photos, portfolio and apprentice evidence images on staging are plain-colour swatches generated by the seed and labelled TEST.

## Stamp set (gumball)

Six postage-stamp-style designs (`flash/stamps/stamp-*.png`) are original vector artwork generated for Rebellious Ink by `scripts/stamp-flash.mjs` on 22 October 2026. No third-party images or fonts beyond the system sans-serif. Rights: the studio.

## House flash (catalogue)

Ten images under `flash/house/` (five subjects in colour and black and grey) supplied by the studio on 28 October 2026 as its own artwork. Rights: the studio. The archive catalogue designs are retired but kept on record.

## Connor's portfolio

Nine photographs under `portfolio/connor/` supplied by the artist via the studio on 29 October 2026, selected from 36 in his shared folder. Rights: the artist; publication permission recorded against each image on the studio's instruction. Shots with clearly identifiable client faces were left out.
