# Chair-Rent Artist Licence

> **DRAFT for studio review. Not legal advice. Have a solicitor review before signature.**
>
> v0.1, 22 October 2026

*Rebellious Ink. For a self-employed tattoo artist who pays fixed rent and keeps their own takings.*

### Notes for the studio (delete this box before signing)

- **Use this for** a tattoo artist who pays a fixed weekly or monthly chair rent and keeps 100% of what Clients pay for their tattooing. It is the arrangement that fits self-employed status best, because the Studio takes no share of the artist's income.

- **Keep the rent fixed.** If rent starts to vary with takings, or the Studio takes any percentage of the artist's work, the arrangement stops being chair rent. Use agreement 02 instead and think about status again.

- **Daily settlement.** There is no commission here, so clause 6 mostly works in the artist's favour (the Studio passes on Deposits it holds). It still applies to any sum shown as due to the Studio. The Studio should decide whether a daily statement fits a chair-rent artist, because the more rigid the accounting, the less this looks like a licence to rent a chair.

- **Decide:** who bears card and payment fees on Deposits; whether VAT is added to the rent (the studio's records say it is not VAT registered, to be confirmed by the accountant); whether to offer the optional rent break.

- **Before signing:** run the arrangement through HMRC's Check Employment Status for Tax (CEST) tool and keep the answers; have the accountant confirm the tax and VAT treatment; have a solicitor review. See `00-how-these-contracts-work.md`.

**Placeholders.** `[CAPITALS]` is information the studio must fill in (names, amounts, percentages, dates). `[24]` or `[£5 million]` is a proposed figure: keep it or change it. `[to confirm]` marks a term taken straight from the owner's brief: please confirm it. `**[OPTIONAL ...]**` marks a clause the studio may delete after advice.

## 6. Daily settlement and payments

**6.1** At the end of each day on which the Artist works at the Premises, completes a session, or takes a payment from a Client for work at the Premises, the Studio System produces a **Daily Statement**. It shows:  
(a) each piece worked on that day, with its price and the verified Deposit applied;  
(b) the balance taken from the Client on the day, and who took it (the Studio or the Artist);  
(c) Deposits and balances the Studio holds for the Artist's completed sessions, less payment fees if the Schedule says so;  
(d) tips taken through the Studio, which the Studio passes to the Artist in full (less card fees if the Schedule says so);  
(e) Stock Items issued and retail sold that day;  
(f) any Rent or other Sums Due that fall due that day; and  
(g) the net amount to be settled, and in which direction.

**6.2** The Artist checks the Daily Statement and records in the Studio System, before Close of Business, any payment they took directly from a Client (for example cash).

**6.3** **Settlement by Close of Business.** Every sum shown on the Daily Statement as due to the Studio must be settled with the Studio through the Studio System by Close of Business on the same day [to confirm]. The Artist pays by bank transfer or card through the Studio System, or by handing cash to the Studio Manager (or placing it in the Studio's safe with the Daily Statement slip) and recording it. A payment is on time if it has been sent, or handed over and recorded, by Close of Business.

**6.4** **What the Studio pays.** Every sum shown as due to the Artist (for example Deposits and balances the Studio took for the Artist's completed sessions, and tips) is paid by the Studio by the end of the next Working Day [to confirm the timing]. The Studio may net sums due in both directions on the Daily Statement.

**6.5** If the Artist is not at the Premises on a day when a sum falls due, they settle it by bank transfer by Close of Business that day.

**6.6** The Artist may query any Daily Statement within [2] Working Days. The Artist pays the undisputed part on time. The parties correct mistakes in either direction, and clause 20 applies to any query they cannot settle.

**6.7** **If a day is missed.** If a sum due is not settled by Close of Business, then:  
(a) interest runs on it under clause 6.9;  
(b) the Studio Manager will contact the Artist on the next Working Day;  
(c) the Studio may set off the unpaid sum against any money the Studio holds for, or owes to, the Artist (Deposits, balances, tips);  
(d) if the sum is still unpaid [7] days after it fell due, the Studio may suspend the licence by written notice under clause 4.6 until the sum is paid in full with interest (clause 6.8); and  
(e) if it is still unpaid [14] days after it fell due, the Studio may end this Agreement under clause 4.4.

**6.8** **Suspension for non-payment.** The suspension in clause 6.7 begins when the Studio sends the written notice and ends on the next Working Day after the Artist pays what is owed with interest.

**6.9** **Interest.** Interest on any late Sum Due runs daily from the day after the due date until payment, at the rate set under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998 (at the date of this draft, 8% a year above the Bank of England reference rate). The parties agree that interest runs at that rate whether or not the Act applies by its own force. **[Solicitor to confirm whether the Act applies to this arrangement, and whether to add the fixed sums of compensation the Act allows.]**

**6.10** The Artist records every price and payment accurately and in full. Deliberately under-recording a price or payment, or taking payment for work at the Premises outside the Studio System, is a serious breach. The Studio may check the Studio System records against Deposits, payment provider records and Client confirmations. The Studio will not ask to see the Artist's private bank records.

**6.11** The Studio issues invoices or statements for Rent and Stock Items as the Studio System produces them. The Artist keeps copies for their own tax records.

**8.8** **Respect and access.** The Artist treats Clients and the Studio's staff with respect and makes the reasonable adjustments for access needs that are recorded in the Studio System. Both parties comply with the Equality Act 2010.

**8.9** **Aftercare and touch-ups.** The Artist gives the Client the Studio's aftercare guidance for the procedure. (c) If one person is in both, each party stays responsible for the data it holds.

**9.7** **No copying.** The Artist must not copy, export, download, photograph, screenshot or otherwise take any part of the Studio's client records or client database, or use it to contact Clients for the Artist's own or another business. A Client may choose to follow or contact the Artist. The Artist may give their own details when asked, and may reply to a Client who contacts them.

**9.8** **Security.** The Artist uses only their own login, never shares it, keeps their devices locked, and tells the Studio at once (and in any case within [24] hours) of any loss, breach or wrong access. Health information is special category data. It is shared only on a need-to-know basis and never in messages, social media or personal chat apps.

**9.9** **Photographs.** The Artist photographs or films a Client only with the Client's permission, and uses the image only for the purposes the permission covers, as recorded in the Studio System.

## 12. Conduct on the Premises

**12.1** **Alcohol and drugs.** The Artist does not drink alcohol or take illegal drugs on the Premises or before working, and does not work while impaired by alcohol, drugs or medicine. Prescribed medicine taken as directed is fine, so long as it does not impair the Artist. There is no smoking or vaping inside the Premises.

**12.2** **Under-18s: tattooing.** The Artist never tattoos anyone under 18 (Tattooing of Minors Act 1969). Parental consent is not an exception. The Artist checks photographic ID for every Client at every appointment.

**12.3** **Piercing age.** The Artist does not carry out piercing at the Premises unless their Registration, insurance and the Schedule all authorise it. Where they do, the Artist pierces only Clients who meet the Studio's piercing age policy in the Schedule, including any ID and parental requirements in it.

**12.4** **Unwell.** The Artist does not work while they have a condition that could be passed to a Client or colleague, or that stops them working safely. Examples are vomiting or diarrhoea (until 48 hours clear), an infectious skin condition, cold sores or open sores near the work area, a feverish respiratory infection, or uncovered cuts or weeping skin on the hands. The Artist tells the Studio Manager they cannot work so that Clients can be rebooked, but need not give medical details. The Studio keeps any health information confidential.

**12.5** **Hepatitis B.** The Artist gives the Studio evidence of hepatitis B vaccination, or a signed declination, before starting. **[Studio to decide whether to require this.]**

**12.6** **Safety.** The Artist reports accidents, near misses, sharps injuries and incidents to the Studio Manager at once and follows the Studio's exposure and fire procedures. The Studio deals with any report to the authorities that the law requires.

**12.7** **Respect.** The Artist does not harass, bully, discriminate against or threaten anyone, and does not film or photograph people without their permission.

**12.8** **Visitors.** Only Clients, and the guests the Studio's policy allows, are in treatment areas. Other visitors to the Station need the Studio Manager's agreement.

**12.9** **House rules.** The Studio may set reasonable written house rules about safety, security, hygiene, noise and shared spaces, and will tell the Artist of them in writing. They apply to everyone on the Premises. They do not govern how the Artist designs or carries out their work.

**12.10** **Security.** The Artist locks up and sets the alarm when they are last to leave, and does not stay on the Premises overnight.
