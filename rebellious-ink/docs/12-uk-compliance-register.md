# UK compliance register (21 October 2026)

Everything in the product is built for England and Wales law and UK terminology. This register lists, feature by feature, the UK law or guidance it rests on, what the software enforces, and what remains the owner's responsibility. It has not been reviewed by a solicitor; the "Owner to confirm" column says where legal or professional advice is still needed. Nothing here is US-based.

| Area | UK basis | What the software does | Owner to confirm |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tattooing age | Tattooing of Minors Act 1969 | Under-18 tattoo enquiries are refused at the form and at hold time; parental consent is not accepted; no setting can override; photographic ID checked in studio is stated on every public page | Keep ID checking at every appointment |
| Piercing age | No national minimum for most piercings in England; Public Health (Wales) Act 2017 bans intimate piercing under 18 in Wales; studio policy | Minimum age is a setting (default 18) and the public guides say so | Decide the under-18 piercing policy with parental presence rules, and whether any Welsh client work applies |
| Registration of studio and practitioners | Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1982 Part VIII and the council's byelaws | Apprentice catalogue makes council registration a core prerequisite before any work on the public; licence and insurance dates are shown on the Artists page | Register every practitioner, apprentices included; display certificates in the studio |
| Health and safety | Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974; COSHH Regulations 2002; RIDDOR 2013 | Catalogue items for COSHH sheets, standard infection control precautions, sharps and exposure incidents and RIDDOR reporting; the exposure control plan is a signed prerequisite | Keep written risk assessments and the exposure control plan current |
| Clinical waste | Environmental Protection Act 1990 duty of care; Hazardous Waste Regulations 2005 | Catalogue item for the registered waste carrier, consignment notes and records | Use a registered carrier; keep consignment notes for the required period |
| Infection control training | Council registration conditions; recognised Level 2 infection prevention and control courses | Core prerequisite with certificate evidence; renewal is a Stage 5 item | Choose the course the council accepts |
| Hepatitis B | NHS occupational guidance for exposure-prone work | Core prerequisite with vaccination record evidence | Occupational health arrangements |
| Personal data | UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018 | Privacy notice cites the lawful bases; health answers sit in a restricted schema with access logging; image publication needs a recorded permission per purpose; clients can withdraw marketing consent from their portal | Confirm retention periods with the insurer and council and publish them; register with the ICO and pay the fee |
| Health data | UK GDPR Article 9 (special category) | Health questionnaire answers are stored separately and never shown in staff chat, email previews, social tools or AI summaries | Decide the Article 9 condition with advice (explicit consent is the design assumption) |
| Marketing email | Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003; UK GDPR | Campaigns go only to clients whose latest preference is a grant; every email carries a one-click unsubscribe; opt-in is recorded with source and version | Keep the "soft opt-in" question in mind if you ever email past clients who did not tick the box: the software does not do that |
| Pricing and urgency | Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024 (drip pricing and fake urgency); Consumer Protection from Unfair Trading Regulations 2008 | Flash prices are the total price with the deposit deducted; no fees are added later; countdowns and counters in later steps must use real server data; promotions have genuine dates | Keep any in-studio price boards consistent with the website |
| Deposits and cancellation | Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013; Consumer Rights Act 2015 | Deposit terms are shown before payment and stored with the quote version; the 48-hour reschedule rule is a setting | Confirm with advice whether the 14-day cancellation right applies to dated appointments booked online and adjust the terms text if it does |
| Card payments | Payment Services Regulations 2017 (via Stripe as the regulated provider); PCI DSS handled by Stripe Checkout | Card details never touch the site; webhooks are signed and idempotent | Stripe account in the studio's legal name |
| Games and promotions | Gambling Act 2005 (free-entry promotions are not gambling when no payment is required to play and nothing of money's worth is won by chance); CAP Code for promotions | Every game is free to play, leads only to a normal fixed-price booking, and awards no prize. The gumball machine shows the £30 total before the spin, charges nothing to spin, picks on the server, shows a real stock count, and age-gates at 18 | Ask the Gambling Commission or a solicitor before launch if any paid element is ever added; it is not planned |
| Advertising | UK Advertising Codes (CAP and BCAP), ASA | Promotion ribbons and banners show dates and terms; social posts need client permission on record | Nothing further |
| Accessibility and equality | Equality Act 2010; WCAG 2.2 as the technical reference | Access needs are captured and shown to staff; 44px targets, labels on every control, keyboard operation, reduced-motion support in later game steps | Physical access arrangements |
| Apprentices | Apprenticeship agreements and the National Minimum Wage apprentice rate where the person is an employee; HMRC rules on self-employment | The portal records hours, skills and sign-off; it does not decide employment status | Agree the apprentice's contract and pay with an accountant or adviser |
| Artist arrangements | HMRC employment status rules; VAT Act 1994 | Chair rent and commission models are recorded with effective dates; VAT registration is a setting | Accountant to confirm the VAT and employment treatment |
| Loyalty and referrals | Consumer protection rules on promotions; no cash alternative | Credit has no cash value, is non-transferable, and is only earned on completed paid appointments | Nothing further |
| Records retention | Council registration conditions and insurer requirements | Consent records and signatures are immutable once created; audit log is append-only | Publish the retention period once confirmed |

## Terminology

UK English throughout the interface and documents: colour, licence (noun), programme, jewellery, sanitiser, steriliser, standard infection control precautions, registered waste carrier, council registration. Currency is GBP; dates are day/month/year in Europe/London.
