# Video 01 — The Launch Video (Intro)

## Concept
"Old dog, new tricks" origin story. Hook them with the vulnerability, keep them with the relatability.

---

## HOOK (first 3 seconds — make or break)

**On screen text:** "I'm 48. I just decided to learn AI. Everyone thinks I'm insane."

**You say (to camera, casual, no teleprompter energy):**
> "I'm 48 years old. And I just decided to completely change my career — into AI."

---

## BODY (15–30 seconds)

> "Here's the thing nobody tells you about being almost 50 and wanting to start over:
> everyone around you gets really quiet.
>
> Not mean. Just... quiet.
>
> Like they're trying to figure out how to say 'that's not how this works' without hurting your feelings.
>
> There's this saying — you can't teach an old dog new tricks.
>
> I want to find out if that's actually true.
>
> So I'm documenting every single thing. The wins, the confusion, the moments I feel completely out of my depth.
>
> No filters. No pretending I already know what I'm doing."

---

## CALL TO ACTION (last 3–5 seconds)

> "Follow along if you're also trying to figure out if it's too late — because I don't think it is.
> But we're about to find out."

**On screen text:** "Follow for the full journey. 48 & learning AI."

---

## CAPTION (TikTok post copy)

```
48 years old. New career. AI. Everyone thinks I'm crazy.

Maybe they're right. Let's find out. 🤷

#CareerChange #LearningAI #48AndStartingOver #NeverTooLate #AIJourney #TechCareer #OldDogNewTricks #LifeAt48
```

---

## PRODUCTION NOTES
- Film handheld, eye level, natural light — authenticity over polish
- No background music for the hook, add subtle lo-fi under the body
- Keep cuts tight, no pauses longer than 1 second
- End with a zoom-in on your face for the CTA line
- Ideal length: 30–45 seconds
