# TikTok Content Strategy
## "48 & Learning AI" — Career Change Documentary Series

---

## The Core Angle

You are NOT a tech influencer. You are a real person, mid-life, taking a genuine risk.
That is the content. Don't perform expertise you don't have — perform the *journey*.

**The audience:** Other people 35–55 who feel like the world is moving without them,
who wonder if it's too late, who are curious about AI but intimidated by it.

**The promise:** Radical honesty. If you're confused, say so. If something clicks, celebrate it.
If you fail, film it.

---

## Series Pillars (rotate between these)

| Pillar | Description | Example |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| **The Struggle** | Real-time confusion, mistakes, overwhelm | "I just spent 3 hours on something a 22-year-old did in 10 minutes" |
| **The Wins** | Small breakthroughs, no matter how minor | "I built my first AI prompt that actually worked" |
| **The Research** | What you're learning, explained simply | "What even IS a large language model? Here's how I understand it now" |
| **The Reality Check** | Industry truth, job market, what's actually needed | "I asked 5 AI companies what they actually hire for" |
| **The Community** | Responding to comments, others on the same path | Stitch/duet with people who relate |

**Body:**

>
> So here's what I'm doing. I'm picking ONE thing and starting there.
> I'm not going to learn everything. I'm going to learn enough to get hired.
>
> Today I'm choosing [your first tool/course/resource].

## Posting Cadence

- **First 2 weeks:** Post every day (build momentum, feed the algorithm)
- **After that:** 4–5x per week minimum
- **Best times:** 7–9am, 12–1pm, 7–9pm (your audience's timezone)
